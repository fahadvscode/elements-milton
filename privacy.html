Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 26, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how this independent information and registration website for Elements Milton ("this site") collects, uses, and protects information submitted by visitors.

What we collect

When you submit the registration form, we collect your first name, last name, email address, and phone number, along with consent status, the page you submitted from, and standard marketing attribution data (such as UTM campaign parameters) where available.

Why we collect it

This information is used solely to contact you about Elements Milton and similar pre-construction opportunities that may be of interest, in accordance with the consent you provide at the time of submission.

How it is stored

Submitted information is stored securely with Supabase, a third-party data processor, using row-level security controls. Analytics on this site are processed via Google Analytics 4 and Meta Pixel, which may set cookies in your browser to measure site usage and advertising performance.

Your consent and how to withdraw it

You provide express consent (in compliance with Canada's Anti-Spam Legislation, CASL) at the time you submit the form. You may withdraw consent at any time using the unsubscribe link included in any message you receive.

Requesting deletion

To request that your information be deleted from our records, contact us using the contact details available on this site, or reply "unsubscribe" to any message you receive. We will process deletion requests within a reasonable timeframe in accordance with PIPEDA.

Cookies and analytics

This site uses cookies for Google Analytics 4 and Meta Pixel to understand site usage and measure marketing performance. You can control cookies through your browser settings.

Compliance

This site's data practices are intended to comply with Canada's Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA) and CASL.
