Elements Milton is a pre-construction freehold townhome community by Trinity Point Developments, a division of Greenpark Group, located at Bronte Street South and Britannia Road in Milton, Ontario. The release comprises 60 double-car-garage freehold towns ranging from 3 to 5 bedrooms, priced from the $600s, with registration open ahead of a planned October 2026 launch.
