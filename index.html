Elements Milton brings a boutique release of 60 freehold townhomes to one of Milton's most established residential corridors, at Bronte Street South and Britannia Road. Every home is planned with its own double-car garage — a rare feature at this price point in Halton Region — across a mix of 3, 4, and 5 bedroom layouts designed for growing families and downsizers alike.

The site sits within walking distance of established neighbourhoods and a short drive from Milton's downtown core, positioning the community for buyers who want small-town character with fast highway and GO Transit access. Trinity Point Developments, operating as a division of Greenpark Group, brings the backing of an established Ontario homebuilder to the release.

Milton has been one of the fastest-growing municipalities in the Greater Toronto Area for over a decade, and the town's own growth plans point to continued demand for freehold product in this segment. Elements Milton is positioned to capture buyers priced out of Oakville and Burlington while staying inside the GO Transit and 401/407 corridor.
